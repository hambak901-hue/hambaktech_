## 2. Service Availability & Delivery Model

- **Nearby Local Services:** Walk-in customers and local clients within Ibeju-Lekki / Lekki LCDA access direct counter and workstation services at standard rates.
- **Long-Distance & Regional Delivery:** Physical goods (printed materials, custom stationery, laminated cards) ordered from distant locations attract distance delivery fees calculated dynamically at checkout.
- **Digital Fulfillment:** Remote services (CAC filings, NIN verification, VTU airtime/data, web design consultations, online academy courses) are fulfilled 100% digitally through the platform.

---

## 3. Dynamic Pricing Policy

> **MANDATE:** All prices across all 14 divisions must be database-driven and dynamically configurable by HambakTech administration. No permanent hardcoded prices are permitted in any user-facing frontend component.

1. Base prices, promo discounts, distance shipping rates, and agent commission tiers are stored in the database.
2. Price adjustments by the business owner take immediate effect without redeploying frontend code.
3. The platform provides clear receipt itemization showing base service fee, applicable VAT/charges, and delivery fee (if physical shipping is requested).
